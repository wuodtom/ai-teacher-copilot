# AI Teacher Copilot

**Plan less. Teach more.**

AI Teacher Copilot helps teachers generate lesson plans, quizzes, rubrics, differentiation strategies, and parent messages in minutes — so they can spend their energy on teaching, not paperwork.

Built by a practising teacher, for teachers.

---

## What it does

Pick what you need, enter your topic and year group, and get a ready-to-use, fully editable draft you can copy or print:

- **Lesson Plans** — objectives, success criteria, starter, main activity (with Engineering Design Process stages), plenary, vocabulary, and resources.
- **Quizzes** — mixed-format questions with space for a mark scheme and answer key.
- **Rubrics** — clear assessment grids across four performance levels.
- **Differentiation** — concrete support and stretch strategies for EAL, SEN, and high-ability learners.
- **Parent Messages** — warm, professional drafts ready to personalise and send.

Every output is editable in the browser before you copy or print it — nothing is locked.

---

## How to use it

1. Open the live site (see below).
2. Choose a tool from the tabs (Lesson Plan, Quiz, Rubric, Differentiation, Parent Message).
3. Enter your topic, year group, and subject.
4. Click **Generate**.
5. Click any text to edit it, then **Copy** or **Print / Save as PDF**.

No login, no installation, works on any device with a browser.

## Roadmap

This is **v1** — a working tool that delivers structured, editable drafts instantly with no setup.

Planned next:

- **v2 — Live AI generation.** Connect an AI model so outputs are uniquely generated for each prompt, not template-based.

- More tools: marking feedback, knowledge organisers, and revision booklets.
